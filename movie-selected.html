John Wick 4

2h 49m 2023 USA

about John Wick 4

With the price on his head ever increasing, legendary hit man John Wick takes his fight against the High Table global as he seeks out the most powerful players in the underworld, from New York to Paris to Japan to Berlin.

Development of the fourth John Wick film was confirmed before the release of its predecessor. In May 2019, it was announced that the film would be written by Derek Kolstad, with Chad Stahelski returning as director. Filming began in June 2021, and filming locations included France, Germany, New York City, and Japan.
